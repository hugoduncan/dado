# Dado Project Glossary

## Project

A set of files specifying the goals, design and implementation of a
system, including all software created to meet the goals. The project
evolves over time through increasing both specificity (moving from
abstract goals to concrete implementation) and precision (clarifying
intent and removing ambiguity at each level of specificity).

- Refining abstract goals into concrete features
- Clarifying ambiguous requirements
- Adding detail at each level of abstraction
- Recording decisions that increase precision or specificity

### Project Goal
A specific, desired outcome or capability of the project. Goals can
exist at multiple levels of specificity and may evolve over time.

### Project Requirement
A concrete feature that the project must support in order to meet its
goals.

### Project Glossary
A definition of the project specific meaning of terms used by the
project.  Terms should be very specific and have little overlap between
terms.

## Information Characteristics

### Information Precision
The degree of clarity and unambiguity about meaning or intent. A precise statement leaves no room for misinterpretation, regardless of its level of specificity. For example, both "the system must be fast" (imprecise) and "the system must respond within 100ms" (precise) could exist at the same level of specificity.

### Information Specificity
The level of abstraction at which information is expressed. Higher specificity means more concrete details, while lower specificity deals with broader concepts. For example, "improve system quality" has low specificity, while the actual code implementing a quality improvement has high specificity. An interface has lower specificity than its implementation.


## Organization Concepts

### Cross-reference
An explicit link between related pieces of information in the system.

### Hierarchical Decomposition
The breakdown of complex concepts or goals into simpler, related components while maintaining clear relationships to parent elements.

### Context Dependencies
The relationships between different pieces of information that must be considered together to maintain consistency and coherence.

### Scope Level
The description of system goals and features without reference to architecture or implementation details.

## AI Interaction Concepts

### Context
Information provided to the AI to support a specific interaction. Includes both the immediate task-specific information and broader system understanding required for coherent responses.

### Completion
The response generated by an AI model for a given prompt/interaction.

### Information Organization
The structured arrangement of system knowledge to optimize AI interactions and system development.

### Interaction
A single exchange between a human and the AI, consisting of provided context and a specific query or task.

### LLM (Large Language Model)
An AI system trained on text data that can understand and generate human-like text. The primary tool for system development in Dado.
